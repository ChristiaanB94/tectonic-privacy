Privacy Policy

App: Tectonic Daily Challenge (com.faultlinepuzzles.tectonic)
Developer: Fault Line Puzzles
Last updated: 17 November 2026

Summary

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or location. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (completed puzzles, crowns, settings and puzzles in progress) is stored locally on your device. We do not receive or store this data. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and app interactions to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised advertising is used. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and Google Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google (AdMob), as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google through advertising, you can use Google's privacy controls (see links above). If you are in the EU, you have rights under the GDPR, including to withdraw consent at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
